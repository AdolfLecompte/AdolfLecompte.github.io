# What's in it

A Quickshell shell (`quickshell/`) providing the bar, the panels and the lock
screen, driven by a Hyprland config written in **Lua** (`hypr/`).

- **Bar** — launcher, notifications, workspaces (incl. special workspaces), media,
  clock, tray, USB, screen recording, active window, keyboard layout, network,
  bluetooth, sound, battery, CPU and memory, screen time, timer, microphone,
  clipboard, screenshot, power. The bar goes on any of the four edges, and every capsule
  can be dragged to either end or its centre; each one chooses how much it
  says — `full`, `compact` or `icon`, offered only where they differ. Four
  shapes for the bar itself — pills, island, solid, and docked (flush with
  the whole edge) — and five
  styles for the workspaces: icons, numbers, dots, Pac-Man or kanji, each with
  an optional tick per window. Special workspaces keep their own icon in all
  of them.
- **Look** — every surface solid or glass (*Blur*: the terminal's own
  translucency, with Hyprland's blur behind), and one outline switch for the
  whole shell or part by part.
- **Dock** — pinned and open applications on an edge of your choosing. A click
  launches, focuses, or hides to a special workspace, in that order; a
  right-click pins or unpins, and Settings has the list with a search. It
  can also carry bar pills as icons (sound, battery, clock…) and shortcuts:
  shell actions like lock or game mode, or a command of your own.
- **Panels** — every pill opens a panel that grows out of it and leaves the same
  way, or opens like a window from the bar's edge. They share one language —
  tinted plates, one radius, one type scale — and never stick out past the bar.
- **Lock screen** — its own `WlSessionLock` surface: padlock, fade or no intro,
  PAM auth, blurred wallpaper, and the desktop's own widgets in three columns
  — music with its sung line, notifications, the machine with its battery
  time on hover — plus a switch in its power menu to show the password as you
  type it.
- **Login screen** — an SDDM theme: one column on the left with the time, one
  plate holding your face, name and password, the three ways out and the
  session, over drawn mountains and spruce groves. One of five palettes
  (`preset=` in its `theme.conf`: dusk, forest, midnight, amber, ash). It
  carries no name, so it suits any desktop.
- **Desktop widgets** — clock, weather, calendar, sun, media, lyrics,
  visualiser, timer, notifications, updates, battery, machine, disks, screen time and
  pictures. Arrange them with `SUPER + SHIFT + D` and drag a widget by any part
  of it; right-click one for its shapes, sizes, the other screen, or to remove
  it.
- **Two screens** — arrange them on a grid, mirror one, or let workspaces spread
  over them (1-5 / 6-10, or evens on one and odds on the other); each bar shows
  its own. Widgets and wallpapers live on a screen of their own, and the whole
  arrangement comes back when a screen is plugged in again.
- **Screen time** — how long each application had you today and across the
  week, counted only while you are at the machine. A panel, an optional pill and
  a desktop widget with each app's own icon; the numbers never leave
  `~/.config/ashen`.
- **Timer** — stopwatch, countdown and a pomodoro (25 min focus, 5 break, a
  long one every fourth) in one panel, from its pill or `SUPER + SHIFT + T`.
  While something runs, a card heads the notifications with its controls; its
  end is a notification of its own.
- **Game mode** — one key flattens the compositor (no animations, blur, shadows,
  rounding or gaps) and quiets the shell, then puts everything back exactly as it
  was, your screen layout included.
- **Wallpapers** — a picker you can search, video or still, and a different
  wallpaper on each screen when more than one is plugged in.
- **Process monitor** — CPU, GPU and traffic drawn as the last minute of samples,
  memory, drives and temperatures as levels.
- **Launcher, clipboard, and Settings with a search** that takes you to the row.
- **Four languages** — English, Spanish, Russian and German, switched on the spot.
  The shortcut list, the welcome and the release notes follow it too; the notes
  are read from `i18n/changelog.<lang>.md` and fall back to English.
- **Dynamic theming** — the wallpaper picker runs `matugen` over the image you
  choose and the whole shell re-colours from `~/.cache/ashen_scheme.json`.
