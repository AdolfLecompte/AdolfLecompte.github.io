# Customize

Almost everything is in **Settings** (`SUPER + I`).

- **Colours** follow your wallpaper. Pick one with `SUPER + SHIFT + W`; light
  mode has its own palettes.
- **The bar** comes in several styles (pills, solid, framed, outlined), can sit
  on any edge, and its pills can be dragged, hidden or parked.
- **Panels** open from their pill in one movement; the panel style is a setting.
- **Desktop widgets** sit over the wallpaper. Arrange them with
  `SUPER + SHIFT + D`; right-click a widget for its own options.
- **Dock**: always, hidden or auto-hide, with the launcher and pills in it.
- **Language**: English, Español, Deutsch, Русский.

Some settings have a command:

```bash
ashen ipc widgets face clock thin
```

Your overrides for Hyprland live in `~/.config/ashen/hypr/` (`layout.lua`,
`border.lua`, `user.lua`) and survive updates.
